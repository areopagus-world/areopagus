# 0004. One voice per space, delegated to chosen leaders

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-14

## Context

Members should have a real say, and active, trusted people should carry more weight. A weight computed by the network from activity measures people and rewards noise. A weight bought with money sells the network.

## Decision

Every confirmed space has one voice. Weight arises only when people entrust their voice to someone they consider a leader. Trust can cover everything or one area, is withdrawn at will, lasts a year, cannot be passed on, and is capped per leader in any one decision. A leader votes in the open; members vote in secret. The core is outside any vote.

## Consequences

- Leaders are made by people, not by a formula.
- A leader's mandate is a number shown where decisions are made. It is not a ranking and is never listed against other leaders.
- The design depends on confirmed identity: fake spaces would be fake voices.
- Delegations are sensitive data and are encrypted at field level.
- The cap (proposed 10%) and the confirmation method are still open.

## Alternatives considered

- **Weight by contribution, capped at three.** Still a network-assigned measure of people.
- **Strict one person, one vote with no delegation.** Simple, but most people will not vote on most questions, and the active few decide anyway without a mandate.
- **Token-weighted voting.** Rejected in the charter.
