# Code of Conduct

Areopagus brings together Christians of different traditions: Orthodox, Catholic, Protestant, Oriental Orthodox and others. We disagree about real things, and some of those disagreements are centuries old. This code exists so that disagreement does not turn into war.

## The principle

"Let your speech always be gracious, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how you ought to answer each person." (Colossians 4:6)

## What we expect

- Speak about other traditions as you would want yours spoken about. Describe what others believe accurately, in terms they would recognise.
- Argue with sources, not with labels. When history or doctrine is contested, cite the text or the document.
- Remember that on this platform every tradition is at home. No tradition is a guest in another's space.
- Assume good faith from people who believe differently.

## What is not acceptable

- Calling another tradition's members non-Christians, heretics or apostates in shared spaces. Doctrinal debate belongs in spaces marked for it, conducted with sources and respect.
- Proselytising members of other traditions who have not asked for it.
- Mockery of another tradition's saints, sacraments, icons, worship or clergy.
- Political content outside the politics section, which is off by default. This includes church-political conflicts between jurisdictions.
- Harassment, threats, doxxing, or revealing anyone's identity or location. Some members live where being Christian is dangerous.
- Any sexual content, and any content that puts minors at risk.

## Contested history and doctrine

Where traditions describe an event differently (1054, the Reformation, the unions, the Crusades), the platform keeps one event with several narratives, each labelled by tradition and supported by sources. Contributions add a narrative or a source; they do not delete someone else's.

## Enforcement

Moderators come from different traditions. A complaint about content from another tradition is reviewed by moderators who do not share the complainant's tradition, or by a mixed panel. Decisions are explained and can be appealed.

Report concerns to conduct@areopagus.world.

## Scope

This code applies to the platform, the code repositories, and any space where people act on behalf of Areopagus.
