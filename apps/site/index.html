Take part

Nobody is ranked here. Every release names all who had a hand in it, in alphabetical order.

  1. Propose

    Add an idea. It appears on the path as an outline next to its branch.

  2. Back it with your voice

    One voice per person per idea. Numbers belong to ideas, never to people.

  3. Take a task

    Translation, testing on your phone, data about your town, code.

  4. Try it first

    Join the circle that gets new things before everyone else.

  5. Support with money

    For one step of the path or for keeping the network running.

Your own domain

Areopagus is also an engine. Your space, shop or community can live on your own domain by subscription. The code is open, so anyone may run it themselves for free.
