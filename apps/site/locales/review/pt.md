Minor issues. The text reads as European Portuguese and the register is consistent, but a few word choices are off or sound translated, and one string changes the meaning.

## Register

The page uses the polite third person ("o seu", "pode", "Acrescente"), with no explicit "você". It is applied consistently throughout. In European Portuguese this is the safe, neutral choice for an audience aged 15 to 60, and it does not come across as corporate. "Tu" would feel warmer to younger readers but could put off older ones, so the current choice is right.
